# CA Final Rank Desk

A single-page study tracker for CA Final (new scheme, all 6 papers).

- `index.html`: standalone version. Open it in a browser or serve it with GitHub Pages. Progress is saved in the browser's localStorage.
- `app.html`: the same page in Claude Artifact format. Signed in, progress syncs to your account.

Features: chapter checklists (Read / Rev 1-3 / Qs, confidence, notes), auto-generated phase timeline,
"due for revision" reminders, daily hours log with heatmap, mock-score tracker with target line,
AIR playbook, and eligibility / resources / exam-day checklists.

Chapter lists and priorities are a study aid. Verify them against ICAI study material and announcements for your attempt.
